### [O-01] AEC Contestant vs. Treaty Marshal
* **Stance:** **AGREE with Codex.**
* **Reasoning:** In Round 0, I over-corrected by turning AEC into a non-combatant NPC gendarmerie to prevent conflict of interest. Codex correctly points out that baseline `04` and `06` treat AEC as a playable third combatant in CASE 4092-B with a distinct defensive/jamming tactical identity. Codex’s minimal repair—allowing AEC to enter with a pre-registered statutory objective (securing neutral communications, evacuation corridors, or infrastructure) while an independent tribunal determines match victory—preserves the three-way battle structure of CASE 4092-B without ethical collapse.
* **Minimal Revision:** Ratify AEC as an active participant in CASE 4092-B whose victory condition is defensive perimeter preservation, adjudicated by an external IMCO tribunal.

### [O-02] Registration vs. Compliance and the "Gray" Label
* **Stance:** **AGREE with Codex.**
* **Reasoning:** Inventing legalistic treaty clauses (e.g., "Article 4 Charters") was unnecessary worldbuilding clutter. Codex’s two-axis matrix—**Registration Status** (Valid / Suspended / Unregistered) vs. **Compliance Status** (Compliant / Under Investigation / Sanctioned)—elegantly explains why Therapia and Noct-Observers can legally dive while being labeled "gray" by field operators and media.
* **Minimal Revision:** Formalize the two-axis system. "Gray" remains an operational and journalistic colloquialism for registered entities suspected of charter violations.

### [O-03] Zone-0 Infiltration Mechanics
* **Stance:** **AGREE with Codex.**
* **Reasoning:** Both models agree that Zone-0 taps downstream telemetry lines of registered participants rather than breaching IMCO’s central vault. Zone-0 divers face constant risk of carrier termination, signal tracing, and fatal feedback without safety ejections.
