## Baseline excerpts

### World and operating rules (`01`–`03`, `10`)

- The world resolves interstate conflicts through dream battles linked to one comatose woman, officially called Observation Subject 01 / “the Girl.” In 2050 she is biologically 28: discovered at age 10 in 2032, isolated by IMCO in 2037, and kept in medical coma. Her dream projection remains 10.
- IMCO has exclusive custody/maintenance of the Girl, receives CASEs, provides the battle framework, and oversees rules. Two to four factions may participate. States contract private divers because compatible divers are rare; coercing operators risks deliberate sabotage in the dream.
- The result of a CASE fixes the disputed rights/territorial outcome, but implementation may involve resistance, delay, or renewed conflict. The Girl does not alter the game board or decide its result. Her limited real-world influence is a low-probability nudge toward a person she can identify through current CASE records; no death, accident, or political outcome is guaranteed.
- AEC is IMCO's enforcement/monitoring force and also appears as a third faction in CASE 4092-B. An independent IMCO adjudication system should determine winners; AEC/field officer Vera may monitor compliance and infrastructure but should not judge her own faction. Violet marks are visible as a local sync display anomaly, not edits to official source records; AEC detects an anomaly but does not know its meaning.
- Deep-dive survivors may become unresponsive patients; AEC's role is to conceal/contain evidence, not to cause the neural injury. Kai's twin sister is such a patient. Whether a dream trace could ever restore a person is not established.
- The tactical architect is an IMCO-managed ARC clone, deployed at age 15 for about 100 days before neural burnout. ARC-Lot.01 is the first deployed lot in 2050. The Girl's dream form is a child; the architect's story should not give him immunity or unique powers beyond the established remote/non-grounded connection.

### Organization baseline (`04`)

The document claims three layers: legal (registered and follows IMCO rules), gray (publicly registered but has covert illegal contracts or forbidden aims), and illegal (unregistered piggyback intruders). It says all factions are balanced in gameplay.

1. **Aegis Task Force (ATF):** legal private military diving company, ex-soldiers, slate gray/rescue orange; heavy armor and logistics; worn tactical clothing, tracked vehicles, container bases.
2. **Vostok Tactics (V-TAC):** legal division of the Vostok conglomerate, snow white/neon cyan; economic growth and drone production; corporate suits and clean geometric buildings/white drones.
3. **Audit & Enforcement Corps (AEC):** legal/IMCO-direct force, pure white/cold silver; public neutrality monitoring and illegal-diver enforcement, but covertly suppresses discovery of the Girl's abuse; jamming and defensive control; mirror-like fortifications.
4. **Therapia Foundation:** gray medical/neuroscience research foundation with roots in the Girl's former clinic; claims patents on life support and synchronization protocol and rights to analyze her data; rumored black-market sample trade; mint/chrome; research/recovery and experimental weapons.
5. **Noct-Observers:** gray registered cult; says it is not interested in state rewards, registers to approach the deep dream and hear the Girl's words; navy/antique gold; stealth/ambush; hooded coats, astronomy jewelry, obsidian towers.
6. **Zone-0:** illegal hacker syndicate and unregistered diver alliance; piggybacks on registered sync lines with modified leaked equipment; also said to be hired covertly by states; black/acid yellow; hacking/raid play; patched suits, masks, noisy weapons.
