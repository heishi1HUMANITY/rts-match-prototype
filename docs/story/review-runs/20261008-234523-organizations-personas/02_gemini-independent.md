### Issue 1.1: AEC and Vera’s Triple Role Collapse (Referee vs. Combatant vs. Cover-up)
* **Baseline Section:** `03`, `06`
* **Nature:** Objective Inconsistency.
* **Failure Scenario:** In CASE 4092-B, AEC participates directly as a third combatant force competing for strategic dispute resolution, while Inspector Vera is simultaneously described as the referee/compliance monitor and the agent suppressing evidence of Subject 01’s abuse. A spectator or reader will immediately ask: *If AEC wins territory or concessions in the dive, who penalizes Vera if AEC cheats? If she enforces penalties, why would V-TAC or ATF accept her rulings while her own unit is flanking them?*
* **Minimum Surgical Repair:** Decouple **Adjudication** from **Enforcement Deployment**.
  1. IMCO’s *Judicial Directorate* (an automated/magisterial arbitration chamber operating outside the arena) handles all scoring, boundary calls, and outcome declarations.
  2. AEC is strictly IMCO’s *field marshaling gendarmerie*. In CASE 4092-B, AEC does not enter as an interested claimant seeking territorial gain; they are an *intervenor unit* deployed under Treaty Article 9 to enforce containment or neutralize illegal participants (e.g., Zone-0).
  3. Vera is not the tournament referee; she is a field Provost Marshal tasked with compliance auditing and physical security.
* **Side Effects & New Assumptions:** Requires a distinct non-combatant adjudicator entity (human or systemic tribunal) in IMCO, freeing Vera to be ruthless on the ground without breaking legal logic.
* **Severity:** **Critical** | **Confidence:** **High**

This section tests the revised design against the seven core worldbuilding rules to verify that no new plot holes or magic exceptions were introduced.

### 1. Who Grants Access?
* **Mechanism:** Access is granted strictly by the **IMCO Judicial Directorate** through cryptographic hardware handshake keys issued to registered cradles.
* **Integrity Check:** Legal factions (ATF, V-TAC) hold combat charters; quasi-legal factions (Therapia, Noct-Observers) hold medical/observer charters; Zone-0 exploits downstream parasitic telemetry leaks on legal lines. No faction enters through hand-waved magic or arbitrary permission.

### 2. Who Pays?
* **Mechanism:** Disputing sovereign states deposit **sovereign treaty bonds into IMCO escrow** prior to the CASE. Winning contractors receive liquidation payouts upon ratification.
* **Integrity Check:** Explains why PMCs (Aegis) risk life and limb, why V-TAC's board demands high ROI, and why dying divers are abandoned when their contract runs out.

### 3. Who Controls the Avatar?
* **Mechanism:** Physical pilots suspended in cold fluorocarbon **induction cradles** project real-time motor impulses through neural interfaces. Avatars mimic pilot training and reflexes.
* **Integrity Check:** If the avatar is destroyed, the pilot experiences severe neural shock. High-tier military cradles (ATF, V-TAC) have explosive-decoupling safety breakers; cheap rigs (Zone-0) risk burning out the pilot's brainstem.

### 4. Who Judges Outcomes?
* **Mechanism:** The **IMCO Judicial Directorate (Chief Adjudicator Zhao)**, using automated server telemetry tracking capture points, sector integrity, and avatar eliminations.
* **Integrity Check:** Completely removes Vera and the AEC from judging. Vera enforces the perimeter; Zhao reads the cold telemetry data and issues the treaty ruling.

### 5. What Records Expose a Target to the Girl?
* **Mechanism:** The Girl does not possess psychic radar. She is exposed **only to data packets routed through her central sensory manifold**.
* **Integrity Check:** When a CASE loads, the biometric telemetry and cryptographic signature headers of all authorized combatants and signatories (e.g., General Brant's authorization seal) pass through her life-support interface. If a header matches an imprint from her childhood trauma (such as the officer who seized her), her brainstem undergoes an involuntary focal seizure, radiating an electrical feedback pulse.

### 6. What Can/Cannot a Dream Trace Do?
* **Mechanism:** A dream trace is **strictly passive sensory memory residue**—a decaying recording left in the comatose medium, like footprints in drying concrete.
* **Integrity Check:** It **cannot** be downloaded into an empty body to resurrect a person. It cannot think, speak autonomously, or grant superpowers. It can only provide raw memory data, emotional catharsis, or proprietary medical biometric profiles. Kai's sister cannot be easily cured.

### 7. What Can Each Faction Plausibly Know?
* **Integrity Check (Knowledge Boundaries):**
  * *ATF:* Knows tactical combat routines and the location of their contracts. Completely ignorant of the Girl’s identity, age, or location.
  * *V-TAC:* Knows market values and technical drone limits. Believes Subject 01 is an advanced proprietary supercomputer mainframe owned by IMCO.
  * *AEC:* Knows Subject 01 is a comatose human being who must be kept secret to prevent war. Ignorant of the fact that her seizures trigger real-world equipment failures.
  * *Therapia:* Knows Subject 01's biological age (28) and medical decay rate. Ignorant of treaty-level covert diplomacy.
  * *Noct-Observers:* Believes Subject 01 is a divine oracle. Ignorant of the technological and corporate infrastructure sustaining her.
  * *Zone-0:* Knows Subject 01 is an abused girl and that divers are being burned out. Ignorant of the global geopolitical consequences if the treaty collapses.

### 8. Added Causal Exceptions Audit:
* **Result:** **Zero new supernatural exceptions added.** The Girl's "nudge" is re-grounded as electromagnetic back-EMF feedback through shared telemetry lines; dream intrusion is re-grounded as parasitic downstream signal tapping; adjudication is restored to a conventional, non-combatant judicial tribunal.
