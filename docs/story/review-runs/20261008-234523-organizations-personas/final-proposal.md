## Agreed organizational framework

- Preserve all six factions and their existing gameplay identities.
- Keep AEC as a potential CASE combatant with a pre-registered neutral-infrastructure objective; give scoring to an independent IMCO adjudicator.
- Separate registration status from compliance status. “Gray” remains an informal label, not a legal tier.
- Make Zone-0's access parasitic and risky; state sponsorship buys illicit information, not legal admission.
- Keep Therapia and Noct-Observers as combat factions while limiting their access to the Girl and distinguishing belief from verified communication.
- Retain the Girl's limited probability nudges, the existing ARC architect role, and the accepted constraints on lost-diver traces and real-world convergence.

## Agreed cast scope

Develop 13 personas in tiers by story function: six faction representatives (Aleksei, Lin, Vera, Kai, Samira, Nadiya); the protagonist and central subject (ARC-Lot.01 and the Girl); institutional decision-makers (Elias Rahal and Sean Hauer); the CASE-linked political figure (Brant); and two limited appearances that show different lost-diver outcomes (Ren and Elena).

Women are 18–29; the Girl remains 28 physically and 10 in the dream as the explicit exception. The user requested an original, industrial near-future atmosphere broadly inspired by *Arknights*, with readable silhouettes and faction-specific materials.
